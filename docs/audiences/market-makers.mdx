# Provide liquidity with collateralized orders

Maker balances track **free base, locked base, free quote and locked quote**. Placing an ask reserves base; placing a bid reserves its quote obligation. A partial fill adjusts the book and the maker's reserved inventory.

![Maker collateral lifecycle](/assets/diagrams/custody.svg)

## Operational workflow

1. Initialize a maker balance for the intended market.
2. Deposit base or quote through the custody vault.
3. Submit a limit order with a validated price and sufficient free collateral.
4. Monitor the order sequence, page index, available liquidity and inventory usage.
5. Read actual fills from confirmed state rather than expecting orders to remain unchanged.
6. Cancel residual quantity to unlock collateral; withdraw only free balances.

## Quoting discipline

Price and raw quantity should be computed using integer arithmetic. Preserve base/quote mint decimal conventions when converting display amounts; bound slippage and account fan-out. High-frequency makers should account for blockhash expiry, RPC variance, transaction ordering and stale snapshots.

## Risk checklist

- A resting order commits inventory even if the wallet token balance remains unchanged.
- Cancel is not proof of withdrawal; re-read the maker balance.
- Use correct page links and owner sidecars when operating across pages.
- Keep keys and signing authority separate from read-only monitoring services.
- Track inventory and failed transactions independently of UI display state.
