# Launch a market on Hybrid

Hybrid provides a Solana program and reference wallet/test infrastructure. Your DEX team still owns deployment authority, market configuration, frontend, token support, customer experience, operational monitoring and incident response.

## Team responsibilities

<Columns cols={2}>
<Card title="Protocol integration" icon="circuit-board">Pin program ID and ABI, provision accounts and keep a verified market manifest.</Card>
<Card title="Trading experience" icon="monitor-smartphone">Wallet connection, quoting, order entry, transaction simulation and receipt inspection.</Card>
<Card title="Custody operations" icon="vault">Manage market authorities, token mints, vaults and maker inventory safely.</Card>
<Card title="Operations" icon="activity">Monitor RPC performance, failed transactions, vault totals and unexpected book state.</Card>
</Columns>

## Launch phases

<Steps>
<Step title="Design">Select token pair, decimals, initialization authority, maker and taker experience.</Step>
<Step title="Provision">Deploy a compatible program; create and validate market, custody, vaults and books.</Step>
<Step title="Integrate">Connect the wallet interface and index/verify the state needed for order UX.</Step>
<Step title="Exercise">Run funded maker/taker lifecycle and independent token/receipt audits.</Step>
<Step title="Operate">Track errors, update authority access, fee balances, reconciliation and recovery processes.</Step>
</Steps>
