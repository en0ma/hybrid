</Warning>

## Selected fixed account sizes

- Market header: **128 bytes**
- Custody state: **192 bytes**
- Maker balance (current custody account): **112 bytes**

The repository enforces persistent account-layout byte budgets in CI. For field-level encoding and compatibility rules, consult `crates/state/src/lib.rs` and `program/src/lib.rs`.

## Ownership checks

Clients and programs must confirm the program ID, SPL Token program, vault mint/authority, market ownership and page/sidecar parallelism. Correct PDA derivation alone is not a complete security check.
