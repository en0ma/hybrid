# Hybrid

A Solana-native trading protocol combining **explicit central-limit-order-book orders** with a **compressed passive-liquidity architecture**.

<Note>
**Current proof point:** The full six-stage funded lifecycle is verified on an isolated **local Surfpool runtime**. This is not a claim of a production mainnet deployment or a completed public-devnet beta.
</Note>

## The lifecycle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Initialize">Create or activate collateralized maker state.</Step>
  <Step title="Deposit">Transfer SPL assets into market vaults and credit free maker collateral.</Step>
  <Step title="Place">Reserve collateral in a price-time ordered resting ask.</Step>
  <Step title="Trade">Execute a taker buy against backed active orders.</Step>
  <Step title="Cancel">Release remaining locked collateral on unfilled quantity.</Step>
  <Step title="Withdraw">Transfer eligible free collateral back to the maker.</Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
The [funded Surfpool CI workflow](https://github.com/en0ma/hybrid/actions/workflows/surfpool-funded.yml) checks six distinct confirmed signatures, receipt linkage, trade settlement, partial book fill, and final balance reconciliation.
</Info>
